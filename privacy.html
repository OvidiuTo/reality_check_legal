Reality Check AI

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

Reality Check AI helps you think through confusing messages, relationship situations, screenshots, and possible red flags. We designed the app around privacy: your checks are tied to your account, protected by Supabase authentication, and are not sold to advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide or generate while using the app, including:

  • Account information, such as your Apple Sign In identifier, email address if Apple provides it, and display name.
  • Onboarding answers, AI tone preferences, and app settings.
  • Reality check content, including text you type, pasted messages, uploaded screenshots, AI results, follow-up questions, and check history.
  • Subscription status and purchase metadata from RevenueCat and Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Technical information needed to run the service, such as request timestamps, error logs, device/app metadata, and usage limits.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide AI analysis, follow-up answers, saved history, and personalization.
  • Authenticate your account and keep your checks connected to you.
  • Manage subscriptions, restore purchases, and enforce paid access.
  • Improve reliability, debug errors, prevent abuse, and keep the app secure.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to support requests.

3. AI Processing

When you ask for a reality check, the text and screenshots you provide may be sent to AI service providers so they can generate the response. Do not upload content you do not have permission to share or content that includes highly sensitive information you do not want processed by an AI service.

4. Screenshots and Sensitive Content

Screenshots may contain names, faces, phone numbers, message contents, locations, or other personal information. You are responsible for deciding what to upload. If you upload someone else's information, you confirm you have the right to do so.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the app, including:

  • Supabase for authentication, database storage, and edge functions.
  • RevenueCat and Apple for subscriptions, purchase validation, and restore purchases.
  • AI providers for generating responses to your checks and follow-up questions.
  • Analytics, logging, or hosting providers used to monitor app reliability and security.

6. Data Retention

We keep account data and check history while your account is active, unless you delete history or delete your account. Some operational records may be retained for a limited period for security, debugging, legal, tax, or compliance reasons.

7. Account Deletion

You can delete your account from the Settings screen. Deleting your account removes your Supabase Auth account and app data connected to it, including profile data, onboarding answers, checks, AI request records, and the subscription mirror stored in our database.

Deleting your account does not cancel an active Apple App Store subscription. You must cancel subscriptions through Apple subscriptions on your device or Apple account.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, or object to certain processing. You can also delete check history or delete your account inside the app.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. No online service can guarantee perfect security, so please avoid submitting information that would put you or someone else at risk if exposed.

10. Children

Reality Check AI is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date and may provide notice in the app or on this page.

12. Contact

Questions or requests can be sent to support@realitycheck.app.

This page is provided for product transparency and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before launch.
